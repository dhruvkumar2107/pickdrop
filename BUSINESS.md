# Business — Bengaluru pilot

Initial segment: SMEs, households, and local retailers moving parcels/small loads within defined Bengaluru zones. Hypotheses: quote transparency and visible matching reduce cancellations; a 15–20% commission can support operations only after measured driver utilization and support cost.

Illustrative per-order model (not a forecast; excluding GST): customer fare ?300; driver payout ?240 (80%); gross platform commission ?60 (20%); payment fee ?6; incentive/support/insurance reserve ?20; contribution ?34. At 15% commission, the same order produces ?19 before acquisition/overhead. Validate actual distance mix, wait time, cancellations, driver payout, payment mix, insurance, claims, CAC and repeat rate in a 4–8 week pilot.

Pilot operations: verify driver DL/RC/Aadhaar/selfie under a documented consent/retention policy; staffed incident/SOS escalation; daily payout reconciliation; OTP plus delivery photo; claims queue; dispatch fallback after timed offers. Track fill rate, pickup ETA, on-time %, cancellation reason, earnings/hour, contribution/job, support contacts/job, damage claims and repeat rate.

Costs at 1k/10k/100k orders are intentionally not estimated without current contracted Maps, SMS, Firebase and payment pricing. Obtain written provider quotes; do not budget from marketing pages. Revenue candidates to validate: commission, transparent surge, business accounts, subscriptions and referrals.
