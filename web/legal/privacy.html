Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what personal data the PickDrop app ("PickDrop", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. PickDrop is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], India.

Data we collect

  • Account: mobile number (used to sign in with a one-time code), name, and optional email.
  • Bookings: pickup, drop and stop addresses and map coordinates, goods description, notes, fare, payment method and trip status history.
  • Location: customers share their location when choosing addresses or triggering SOS. Drivers share their location while online or on a trip so we can match nearby requests and show live tracking.
  • Driver verification: driving licence, vehicle registration (RC), Aadhaar images and numbers, profile photo, and vehicle details. These are visible only to the driver and PickDrop reviewers.
  • Trip photos: goods photos taken by the driver at pickup and drop, visible to that trip's customer, driver and PickDrop staff.
  • Messages and support: in-trip chat between customer and driver, and support requests you send us.
  • Payments: processed by Razorpay. We receive payment status and reference IDs. We do not store card or UPI credentials.
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can send booking updates.

How we use it

To create and fulfil deliveries, match drivers, calculate fares, take payments and pay drivers, verify drivers, resolve disputes and damage claims, respond to safety alerts, provide support, and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell personal data.

Who we share it with

  • The other party on your trip (for example, a driver sees the customer's first name and the pickup and drop addresses; a customer sees the driver's name, vehicle and live location).
  • People you send a live-tracking link to, who see trip status, route and driver location until the link expires or the trip ends.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Google Firebase and Google Maps Platform (hosting, authentication, maps) and Razorpay (payments and driver payouts).
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your profile, saved addresses, emergency contacts and driver documents, and anonymise your user record. Booking, payment and ledger records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

Your choices

  • Edit your name in the app's profile screen.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Delete account.
  • Turn off location or notifications in your phone settings. Some features, such as driving or live tracking, will not work without location.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by account. Driver documents and trip photos can be opened only by the people listed above.
